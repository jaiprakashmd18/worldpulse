        About WorldPulse

        A shared observatory for how we feel

        WorldPulse turns voluntary, anonymous check-ins into a living picture of our planet. No accounts, no names, no tracking of where you are — just one feeling and the country you choose.

        🫧

        Anonymous by design

        We store a mood, the country you pick, an optional moderated note and a server timestamp. Never your name, email, IP address or GPS location.

        🛡️

        Protected small groups

        Country breakdowns stay hidden until at least 10 people have shared in a window, so no one can be singled out.

        📊

        Honest numbers

        Every figure comes from real submissions. When there's no data, we say so. Demo data is always clearly labelled.

        WorldPulse shows voluntary self-reports from people who chose to take part. It is not a scientific survey and does not measure any nation's mental health. A country's results never describe how any individual from that country feels.
